Read down the columns and the ranking is not monotonic — the to-spec code fails a case the no-gap sphere survives. So “the quiet zone is mandatory” was wrong, and so is “it does not matter”: what it really buys is margin, and how much depends on the decoder. This table is OpenCV, which is not what is in your pocket. iOS Camera and Google Lens use different detectors and are generally more forgiving. That is the whole reason these are on a page instead of in a message — your phone is the only test that counts.
