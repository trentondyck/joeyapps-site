Puffers

The shared puffer tracker for families

An empty puffer still sprays, so you have to count. Puffers keeps one puff count for everyone who gives the dose, on every phone in the house.

  • Log a dose once and every phone in the household knows.
  • If a dose isn’t logged within an hour, the other parent gets a heads-up.
  • A chart of your puff history to show the doctor.
  • One subscription covers the whole household. No per-member fees.
  • Free for 30 days, or 12 months free with a note from your child’s doctor.
